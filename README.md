## Key Features

Concrete scenario: Emma, a marketing manager at a mid-sized retail chain, is struggling to maintain her company's e-commerce platform due to rising development costs and limited access to open-source solutions.

She needs to find a way to reduce her e-commerce platform's costs without compromising its functionality. Emma discovers an open-source e-commerce solution, OpenCart, which offers a similar set of features at a significantly lower cost than her current solution. She downloads the OpenCart software and sets it up on her company's servers, which not only reduces her development costs but also allows her team to contribute to the project and build a community-driven ecosystem.
